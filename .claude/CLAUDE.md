# EmpowHER Festival

Marketing site for EmpowHER Festival, a storytelling immersive music production held November 8, 2026, at Austin Garden at Inn Cahoots. Features video hero, live countdown, three-stage lineup, day-of schedule, FAQ accordion, sponsorship tiers, partner and press logo walls, team roster, merch grid, photo gallery, and contact form. Built with Astro, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS. Deployed on Netlify.
