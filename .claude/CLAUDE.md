# EmpowHER Festival

Marketing site for EmpowHER Festival, a storytelling immersive music production by Anna Madewell's Madewell Productions, held April 17, 2027, at Inn Cahoots in Austin, Texas. Features video hero with live countdown, three-stage guide, day-of schedule, FAQ accordion, sponsorship tiers, partner, sponsor, and press logo walls, team roster, photo gallery, merch store with Stripe Checkout, contact and order mail sent through Resend, and custom 404 and 500 pages. Built with Astro, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and GSAP. Deployed on Netlify.
